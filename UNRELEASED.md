# Unreleased

Changes since the last release. Each fix or feature adds a line here; `scripts/release.sh` turns
this list into the release notes and empties it for the next batch.

- Connected and on states are now green in every theme. Warnings and sleeping, off or searching devices are yellow, and devices kit can't find are red.
- NK65 key profiles, like the mouse's: unlimited saved keymaps switched from the tray, a shortcut per profile, a next-profile shortcut or a key on the board.
- New mic test on the Mic page: record a sample and play it back, or listen live, to hear your mic the way apps get it after noise suppression.
